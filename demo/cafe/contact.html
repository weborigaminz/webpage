Good to know

A few quick answers.

If your question isn't covered here, send us a message and we'll point you in the right direction.

How do I enquire about catering?

Choose “Catering enquiry” in the form above and include your date, approximate guest numbers, event type and any dietary requirements. You can also visit the Catering page.

Can I ask about dietary requirements?

Absolutely. Include the details in your message. Any available options and allergen or cross-contact information must be confirmed directly with the cafe before ordering.

Where can I find the menu?

Head to our Menu page for a sample of the food and drinks in this website concept. Final offerings and prices should be confirmed before launch.
