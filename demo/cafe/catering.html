A few helpful details

Before you enquire.

Every event is different. These are some of the details worth discussing with our team.

How far ahead should I enquire?

As early as you can, especially for larger gatherings or popular dates. Your enquiry can include the date and approximate guest count so the team can advise on availability.

Can you accommodate dietary requirements?

Let us know about allergies, intolerances and dietary preferences when you enquire. Options and cross-contact arrangements should be confirmed directly with the cafe before ordering.

Do you cater for different group sizes?

Tell us your expected numbers and event style. The team can discuss suitable quantities and whether your request can be accommodated.

Is delivery available?

Delivery, collection, service areas and any associated fees need to be confirmed with the cafe when you make your enquiry.
