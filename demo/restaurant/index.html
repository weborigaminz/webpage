Welcome to Harbour Table

Gather around the table.

Harbour Table is a neighbourhood restaurant inspired by the coast, seasonal produce and the simple pleasure of sharing a meal.

From long lunches to late dinners, our kitchen celebrates honest ingredients, thoughtful cooking and the people around the table.

Our Story

A restaurant shaped by the harbour.

Harbour Table began with a simple idea: create a place where people could eat well, slow down and enjoy being together.

Our kitchen follows the seasons, working with local producers and letting great ingredients speak for themselves.
